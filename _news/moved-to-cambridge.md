After almost 5 years of working in the beautiful city of Heidelberg (Germany), the time has come to move on.

I will continue doing my line of research in the same lab ([Saez-Rodriguez Group](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/research/saez/)), but this time as a staff member at EMBL-EBI, located on the Wellcome Sanger Institute campus.

I've been to the campus a couple of times before, and I have to say that it's impressive. The green spaces and the architecture make it a really nice place to visit and walk around. Here are a few pics I recently took. One of my favourite times of day is dusk, when the sunlight interacts with the architecture in ways that bring out its beauty.
