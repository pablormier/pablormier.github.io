I was invited to give a seminar talk at [Karolinska Institutet](https://news.ki.se/calendar/biologically-informed-neural-networks) on biologically informed neural networks, as part of a course organized by Avlant Nilsson ([SciLifeLab](https://www.scilifelab.se/)/[Karolinska](https://ki.se/en)).

It was a really nice experience, and a great chance to catch up and learn more about the amazing work Avlant and his team are doing at the intersection of machine learning and biology. I really enjoyed the discussions with his team, and it was also great to meet and exchange ideas with Erik Hartman (Lund University) and Matthieu Barreau (KTH).

As a bonus, I also had the chance to enjoy a bit of Stockholm, a beautiful city with nice food 😄.

Thanks to Avlant for the invitation!
